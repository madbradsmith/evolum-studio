{# ── FINE PRINT ── #}

The rules of the road

  • Only new signups count. Existing accounts and re-registrations don't trigger a reward.
  • A "stick" means the referred user has been an active paying customer for at least 30 consecutive days.
  • Free-week rewards are stackable and can be redeemed against any active EVOLUM subscription tier.
  • Ambassador and Insider cash payouts require a signed subcontractor agreement + W-9. A 1099-NEC will be issued at year-end for total payouts over the IRS threshold.
  • Cash payout amounts are capped as a percentage of the referred user's tier LTV to keep the program healthy on both sides. Exact numbers are posted on the Ambassador dashboard.
  • Self-referrals, botted signups, and any attempt to game the ladder result in forfeited rewards and possible account review.
