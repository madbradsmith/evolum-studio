Section One

Getting around EVOLUM

EVOLUM has five doors — Filmmaker, Above the Line (producers, DPs, directors), Actor, Investor, Supporter — and each opens onto a different set of rooms. Most users live in one door but can browse the public surfaces of the others.

Start at /filmmaker-workspace. That's your home room. From there you'll see your project slate (or an empty state with "Upload Your First Script"). Each project flows through:

Idea → Script → Pitch Deck → Deal Room → Booking → Production → Deliverables.

You don't have to go in order, but most filmmakers do. The script feeds the deck. The deck feeds the deal rooms. Pledges feed production. If you get lost, every room has a back-to-home link and clear navigation — nothing is more than two clicks away.

Two rooms: /audition (the actor's workspace with sides, prompter, and self-tape recording) and /actor-casting (the public casting board with open roles posted by filmmakers).

Browse Casting to find roles. Use the Audition workspace to prep — mark up your sides, run your lines, and record takes. When you book a role, log it into your Booked Role packet so the prep notes follow you to set.

Start at /investor-deal-room. Every project currently raising appears there with its funding ask, deal type, and pitch link. Click any project to read its pitch. You'll sign a one-page NDA to access the deck, the package, and the filmmaker's contact.

When a project's drive closes, the filmmaker schedules a private Deal Room session with you. It's a real two-party negotiation — term sheets get drafted collaboratively, and your counsel takes it from there.

Start at /supporter-feed. Projects currently raising are listed there with their funding progress, deal type, and pledge link. Click any project to read the pitch and pledge support.

Your pledge is a Letter of Intent, not a payment. If the project hits 100% of its drive, the filmmaker reaches out to close. If it doesn't, the pledge dissolves and no money moves. The first supporter on any project gets the Cherry Breaker badge — the credit given to the one who believed first.

Section Two

For Filmmakers

Upload your screenplay. The deterministic engine analyzes it and generates a multi-slide investor deck — logline, synopsis, key art, comparable films, market analysis, team, budget, ask, return profile. You can regenerate any slide, edit the copy directly, or rebuild from scratch. Export as PDF or PowerPoint.

An end-to-end deterministic read of your script. It surfaces dramatic arc, character notes, structural observations, theme, market comparables, and notes a coverage reader might give. Useful as a private second-pair-of-eyes before you send the script out — or as the foundation for the deck.

From your Project Room, set your funding goal, deal type (equity / revenue share / donation), and closing date. Flip the project to live. Your pitch page goes public at /project-room?id=<your-project-id> and the project appears on the Investor Deal Room and Supporter Feed.

You can run an investor side (NDA-gated, accredited investors) and a supporter side (open, smaller pledges, EP credit for backing) on the same project — most filmmakers run both.

The drive auto-closes. Every pledger gets an email saying "drive closed, your Deal Room is next." You get the closing playbook. Your project moves into the Production phase on your workspace. You schedule Deal Room sessions with each investor, sign real paper with your counsel, and start spending.
