EVOLUM · Sponsorship & Partnership

We don't sell ads.
But we'll work something out.

EVOLUM isn't an ad network. The catalog isn't for sale. Filmmakers' pages don't get pop-ups. Investors don't get retargeted. That's a promise — and it's not going to change. But if you love what we're building and want your name on something we're making, there's a conversation to have.

Here's the deal. We're building a house for independent filmmakers who stopped waiting on Hollywood. The workshop is real. The projects are real. The people we're bringing in are real.

If you want to sponsor a room in that house — a podcast we're making, a specific project's development slate, a festival showcase, a filmmaker cohort — we're open. It's not banner ads. It's your name on the wall of a thing that matters.

Ways to Get Your Name on the Wall
Podcast Sponsorship
Sponsor the Show
We're launching an EVOLUM-produced podcast on the craft of independent film — filmmakers, actors, financiers. Your brand is the presenting sponsor. Read live in-episode by the host, in the credits, on the show page.
Presenting sponsor · Quarterly commitments · Real audio host reads
Project Sponsorship
Back a Filmmaker
Pick a specific project on the slate and be its named backer through development. Your name in the deck, on the sizzle page, in the final credits if it goes to production. The filmmaker knows who took the swing.
Direct-to-project · Custom scope · Filmmaker-negotiated
Cohort Sponsorship
Fund a Filmmaker Cohort
We onboard filmmakers in cohorts — 10 to 20 at a time. Sponsor a cohort's platform subscriptions for six months. Filmmakers who couldn't afford the tool get to build; you get the "presented by" line on every project that comes out of it.
Six-month commitment · Named cohort · Reporting on outcomes
Showcase Sponsorship
Present a Festival Showcase
The EVOLUM festival calendar surfaces the slate for programmers, buyers, distributors. Sponsor a themed showcase — "Female-directed 2026", "Comedy pilots", "Micro-budget features" — and be the presenting brand across every touchpoint.
Themed curation · Time-boxed run · Programmer-facing surface
