The basics

How the trial works

Every plan starts with a three-day trial. You enter your card at signup so the subscription can roll over automatically when the trial ends. Cancel before the trial closes from the billing page and you won't be charged.

Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and are pro-rated. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle. You can change plans as many times as you want from the Billing page.

Cancellation

Cancel anytime from the billing page. Your tools stay active until the end of the current billing period — you don't lose access mid-cycle, and you can reactivate the same account if you come back.

Refunds

Subscriptions are non-refundable for a billing period once a charge has succeeded. If you believe a charge was made in error or you were charged after canceling, contact madbradsmith@gmail.com and we'll review it.

What's NOT included

EVOLUM doesn't take a cut of pledges, investments, or supporter contributions. Those funds move between filmmakers and their backers directly — we are the workshop, not the broker. We also don't sell your data or train external AI models on your screenplays. See the Privacy Policy for what we do and don't do with what you upload.
